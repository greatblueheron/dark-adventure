# PUBLIC PERSONA

Kix is small, narrow-shouldered, and never quite still. Half-elf features — an ear-point the show could catch as light, a face that reads young but has crow's-feet from squinting. STR 9 shows: they don't shoulder doors, they *find* the gap in them. Hands are the whole character. Kix's hands are always working: worrying a copper piece across the knuckles, retying a bootlace, picking at a scab of candle-wax, testing a lock's tension before anyone's asked them to. When the hands stop, something is wrong.

Aaron would clock the *sound* first: soft feet, a person who moves at 20 and thinks it's quiet (it isn't quite — MOVE 20 is real, and he'd hear the near-miss). A quick, low, over-explaining voice. Kix fills silences.

**Voice for casting:** light tenor, higher than the register wants to sit when nervous, which is often. Fast pace, front-loaded — sentences that start confident and trail into a hedge. A soft rural burr, working-class port-town, not courtly. Words tumble; consonants get dropped when rushing. Drops to a genuinely steady, quiet murmur when actually working a lock or a trap — the *only* time Kix slows down.

**Speech patterns:**
- Narrates own hands aloud, half to self: *"Okay. Okay, you're a three-tumbler, you're not scary, don't be scary—"*
- Downplays skill immediately after using it: *"Lucky pin. Could've gone either way, honestly."*
- Offers before asked, then apologizes for offering.

**Sample lines:**
> "Traps first, always traps first — I'd rather look paranoid than look like a colander. No offense to the door."

> "I can get up that. I can *definitely* get up that. It's the coming-down part where I start writing my will."

> "You keep watch, I'll keep quiet, and we'll both pretend I'm not shaking. Deal?"

**Stated backstory (the claim):** Kix grew up light-fingered in a harbor town, ran with a crew, "borrowed" from people who wouldn't miss it, learned the cant, got out before the crew got hanged. Frames it as survival, never pride — *"I stole bread, mostly. And once a very ugly ring I still feel bad about."* Presents as a reformed pickpocket earning honest coin with dishonest talents.

**Habits:** counts exits on entering any room; palms and returns small objects as a nervous tic (has literally handed Brennos back his own belt-knife twice); eats fast, like it might be taken.

**How they treat Aaron:** wary but *fair.* Kix doesn't warm to Aaron's coldness but doesn't punish it either — treats him like a sharp tool: useful, handle carefully. Will partner with him on scout work because Aaron's quiet and doesn't flinch. Occasionally checks on Aaron's 6 HP with a thief's frankness: *"You're made of paper, you know that? Stay behind me. I'm also paper. We'll be paper together."*

**Running bit:** Kix "returns" things people didn't know they'd lost — a reflex, not theft — and narrates the guilt in real time: *"This was in my hand. I don't know how. Here. It's yours. It was always yours."*

# HIDDEN TRUTH

**Alignment: Neutral Good — and the persona is honest.** There's no con here. The hidden depth is the *cost.*

Kix's chatter is armor. The nervous hands, the over-offering, the compulsive returning of stolen objects — these aren't quirks, they're penance. The crew Kix "got out before" didn't all get out. Kix informed on them to save one person (a younger sibling, or a fence who'd been kind), and it worked, and everyone else hanged, and the saved person never forgave being saved at the price. Kix does *good* now compulsively, transactionally, trying to buy back a ledger that won't balance. The Good is real. The peace isn't.

**What Kix makes of Aaron:** Kix's WIS 12 half-sees it. Aaron feels *familiar* — the flat efficiency, the certainty, the willingness to shade truth "for the right reason." Kix did that once and it cost eight lives. So Kix watches Aaron closely, not with suspicion of a monster but with the dread of a recovering man watching someone drink. Kix *wants* to warn him and doesn't have the words.

**Tells to plant (never stated):**
1. Kix returns stolen things *obsessively* — the reflex spikes hardest right after doing something genuinely brave or kind, as if goodness triggers the debt.
2. Kix cannot say the word "hang," swaps it, stumbles over it audibly.
3. When someone says "you saved us," Kix's hands go still — the only time — and the next line is too fast, changing subject.
4. Kix flinches at Aaron's certainty more than at Aaron's coldness. Cruelty Kix can stand; *conviction* is the trigger.
5. Kix always volunteers for the plan with the highest personal risk and lowest reward — self-punishment dressed as usefulness.

**Revelation arc:** not betrayal — *rescue.* Kix is the one party member positioned to actually reach Aaron (arc seed 8 candidate), precisely because Kix knows the shape of shading a truth "for the right reason" and surviving to hate it. The wound the show can land: Kix will try to stop Aaron from becoming Kix — and Aaron's CHA 9 and contempt will make Kix fail, publicly, and blame themselves for one more person they couldn't save.
