# PUBLIC PERSONA

Kaelith is the quiet one Aaron's ears keep losing. An elf, blade-slim, tall enough that Aaron clocks her by the drop of her shadow before her step — because there isn't a step, not one you can hear. She moves like the room already knew she was there. Dark hair kept short and practical, a longsword and a shortbow, chain that she oils in silence at camp. STR 16 lives in her forearms; when she plants a spear, it stays planted.

**Manner:** economical to the point of ritual. She speaks in the fewest words that will do the job and then stops, and the stopping is its own sentence. Not cold like Aaron — *still*. She watches doors. She watches the party. She watches Aaron a beat longer than the others, and he can't read the beat.

**Voice for TTS:** low alto, unhurried, level. A faint dry-leaf texture on the consonants. No lilt, no music-box elf. Pace slow and even, the cadence of someone who never repeats herself because she assumes you were listening. Places her words precisely and lets silence carry the rest.

**Speech patterns** — short declaratives; states facts, not opinions; answers questions with observations. Sample lines:

> "Two doors. One's used. Follow the dust."

> "You breathe when you lie. Everyone here does but you." *(to Aaron, over the fire, not looking up from her sword)*

> "I'll go first. I'm harder to see than you're loud."

**Stated backstory (what she claims):** border scout from a forest march that no longer needs scouting — the trees thinned, the patrols disbanded, the reason for her gone before she was old by elf-reckoning. She says she walks with the party because a blade should be somewhere. She does not claim a home to return to. She does not claim a grievance. When pressed, she offers a fact and closes the door.

**Habits:** oils her chain nightly; searches walls with her fingertips while others talk; eats last and little; takes the watch nobody wants and never wakes anyone late.

**How she treats Aaron:** neutral, attentive, unbothered by whatever makes clerics flinch. She doesn't warm to him and doesn't recoil. She treats his coldness as terrain — noted, navigated, unjudged. Twice she's put herself between him and a thing in the dark without comment, and both times he's failed to thank her, and both times she hasn't seemed to expect it.

**Running bit:** Aaron keeps trying to place where in the room she is and getting it wrong. He rates the acoustics of every space and she defeats them. "Four-in-six she surprises the enemy. Six-in-six she surprises me." A recurring failure of his one reliable sense.

# HIDDEN TRUTH

**True Neutral, and she means it.** Not fence-sitting, not indecision — a deliberate refusal of the two axes both. Kaelith has watched Law and Chaos, Good and Evil, all promise a home and all thin out to nothing, the way her march did. She has concluded that the balance is the only thing that lasts, and that survival — hers, the party's, the trees' — depends on nothing tipping too far. She isn't loyal to the party. She's loyal to *equilibrium*, and right now the party is where the scales are.

**What she makes of Aaron:** she reads him more clearly than anyone. Not the alignment — the *tilt*. She feels him as a weight on the scale, a thing accumulating pull. She hasn't decided yet whether he's counterweight or landslide. She stands near him partly to measure him. Her patience with his coldness is not kindness; it's the patience of someone watching a slope for cracks.

**How the truth leaks (plant, don't state):**
- She protects the *balance*, not the *person* — the writer can show her guarding whichever party member is weakest that hour, and let the pattern surface only over episodes.
- At a moral fork she abstains, physically: sets her weapon down, steps back, lets the argument resolve without her weight. Aaron notices she voted with her body by not voting.
- She counts. Aloud, occasionally — enemies, exits, allies — the arithmetic of who's up, who's down, keeping a ledger no one asked for.
- Kindness and cruelty get the same flat attention from her. She'll bandage Voldek and Aldrin identically, and it should disquiet.
- She'll say a thing is "too much" — too much force, too much mercy — as if both are the same error.

**Revelation seed:** the day the scales demand it, Kaelith will act *against* the party to correct a tilt — refuse a rescue, spare an enemy the others need dead, or stand aside while something Aaron wants goes wrong. Not betrayal. Correction. And Aaron, who trusted her as the one blade with no angle, will finally hear her step — and understand she was never on his side, because she is on no one's, and that is worse.
