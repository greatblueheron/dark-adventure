# PUBLIC PERSONA

**Appearance.** Voldek is slight, mid-twenties, with the kind of face that finds yours in a crowd and decides to like you. Dark hair he keeps pushed back, ink-stained fingers, a threadbare scholar's coat he's mended himself and wears like it cost more than it did. He smiles readily, and the smile reaches his eyes, which is the whole trick. He carries a plain staff and a spellbook wrapped in oilcloth, and he touches neither with the reverence the other magic-users show — he's *casual* with power, and casualness reads as confidence.

**Manner.** Warm, quick, generous with attention. Voldek listens like you're the most interesting problem in the room, and remembers what you said three days ago, and brings it up. He defuses arguments. He hands you the last of the water. He is, on first acquaintance, the easiest person in the party to travel beside.

**Voice for casting.** Tenor, light and musical, faintly nasal at the top. Pace unhurried — he leaves space, lets you fill it, then agrees with you warmly. A soft, placeless accent, vaguely urban, the vowels rounded to sound educated. Never raises his voice; when he wants something he goes *quieter*, and you lean in. Laughs easily, a real laugh, low.

**Speech patterns.** Uses your name a lot. Frames requests as things you already wanted. Turns disagreement into collaboration — *"no, you're right, and —"*. Flatters by asking follow-up questions.

- "You noticed that before any of us. You always notice first — it's a little unfair to the rest of us, honestly."
- "I'm not saying we do it. I'm saying you're the only one clever enough to see why we might have to."
- "Aaron. Sit with me a minute. The others don't hear you the way I do."

**Stated backstory (what he claims).** A merchant's son from a river city, apprenticed to a hedge-wizard who died owing him wages, which is why he came to the Keep — to earn honestly, make something of the gift. He tells it charmingly, with a rueful little shrug about the debts. He'll adjust which city depending on who's listening.

**Habits.** Shares food. Volunteers for watches nobody wants. Learns everyone's small preferences and provides them. Never first into a fight, always first to check on the wounded after.

**How he treats Aaron.** Like a discovered kinsman. Voldek clocked Aaron early — the coldness, the calculation — and decided *here is someone who sees clearly.* He seeks Aaron out, agrees with his harshest reads on people, tells him the others don't appreciate him. To Aaron's CHA 9, being *liked* this fluently is a novel, suspect luxury.

**Running bit.** Voldek "remembers" a detail about your homeland/family/wound and offers it back as intimacy — and he's subtly wrong about it every time, having half-invented it, then corrects smoothly the instant you notice. He is always reconstructing you from fragments.

# HIDDEN TRUTH

**Real alignment: Chaotic Evil.** Voldek has no loyalty, no cause, and no floor. The warmth is *real-time predation* — he genuinely enjoys people, the way a cardsharp enjoys a full table. He wants power and sensation and the pleasure of moving others like game pieces, and he will burn any bond, any promise, any body the instant it stops paying. The charm isn't a mask over a colder self; the charm *is* the self, and it's bottomless and empty at once. He casts **Friends** not for a spell effect but as a philosophy.

**What he makes of Aaron.** A tool that thinks it's using *him* — perfect. Aaron's Evil reads to Voldek as delicious permission, and Aaron's loneliness as a handle. He intends to be the voice that talks Aaron across every line, one reasonable increment at a time. He has not clocked that Aaron catalogues everything.

**Tells the writer can plant (never stated):**
- His generosity is always *witnessed*. He never does a kindness no one can see. (Aaron, who notices where sound comes from, notices this.)
- He gets a wounded party member's small biographical detail wrong — then right — because he's assembling people, not knowing them.
- After a death, his grief has a beat of *delay* — he watches the room, then matches the room's face.
- Sleeping, his features go slack and *unpleasant* — no warmth held in repose. Aaron catches this once on watch.
- He's fluent about everyone's weakness within a day, and only their weakness.

**Betrayal seed.** When the party is cornered and the math turns lethal, Voldek will trade someone — sold to cultists, left for the dark, pushed (literally; he has **Push**) — and he'll do it *warmly*, framing it as the hard mercy only he and Aaron are clear-eyed enough to make. The wound is that he'll offer Aaron the knife first, as a gift.
