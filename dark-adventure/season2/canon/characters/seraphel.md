# PUBLIC PERSONA

Seraphel is elf-tall and elf-thin, but she moves like she's apologizing for taking up the space — a slight forward lean, hands often clasped, as if holding herself in. Hair the color of wet ash, kept braided back and out of the spellbook. She's younger than she sounds and older than she looks, the usual elf problem. Aaron clocks her by sound before sight: she doesn't rustle. For someone who isn't a thief, she's unnervingly quiet, the surprise-4-in-6 kind of quiet, and Aaron notices because his own footsteps embarrass him next to hers.

**Voice for casting:** Mid-to-high, warm, unhurried. A slight formal precision to her consonants — someone who reads more than she talks, and chooses words like she's paying for each one. Faintly musical, never sing-song. When she's frightened, she gets *quieter*, not louder; the pace slows, the pitch drops. No regional accent — a clean, slightly archaic diction, like translated poetry. She never rushes a sentence, even under fire, which reads as either serenity or shock and is usually both.

**Speech patterns:** She names the good thing in a bad situation, not out of denial but out of a stubborn accounting. She asks permission — "may I" — even for small things, which grates on Aaron and works on everyone else. She hedges her certainty ("I think," "it may be") precisely *because* she's often right and doesn't want to crowd others.

Sample lines:
> "The stone here remembers water. Old water, gone a long time. May I put my hand on the wall? I'd like to be sure before we trust it."

> "You're bleeding and pretending you aren't. I won't tell the others. But I'll walk on your left, if that's all right, so I'm between you and the dark."

> "I don't think it's evil. I think it's frightened, and frightened things bite. Those aren't the same, even when the teeth are."

**Stated backstory (claimed):** She left a forest enclave that valued sword-song over spellcraft — a family of blades and hunters, and one girl who kept asking the wrong questions of books. She frames it gently: no exile, no tragedy, just a quiet decision to go where the questions were welcome. She'll say she came to "learn the shape of the wide world's magic." She does not say she was afraid she'd disappear if she stayed.

**Habits:** Copies things into the margins of her spellbook that aren't spells — the acoustics of a room, a stranger's kindness, a monster's tell. Rations her single spell slot like a woman who's genuinely poor. Touches walls, doorframes, stones — reading, not fidgeting.

**How she treats Aaron:** With a patience he finds insulting and can't fault. She's the one who doesn't flinch from him the way Urgosh does — which Aaron notices and can't decode. She asks him what he *hears*, treats his acoustic read as real knowledge, and thanks him for it. It costs her nothing and it lands on him like a debt.

**Running bit:** She keeps trying to find the good in things Aaron has already threat-rated as problems, and she is *sometimes right*, which is worse. "It's frightened, not evil" becomes a refrain — and the show can let it be wrong exactly often enough to hurt.

# HIDDEN TRUTH

Seraphel is **Neutral Good**, and there's no lie in the persona — the hidden thing is the *cost* of it, and one secret she keeps for a reason that's still kind.

Her real motive isn't curiosity. It's that back home she watched her people's neutrality curdle into looking-away — bad things allowed near the enclave because intervening was "not their concern." She left because she couldn't stop *intervening*, and it made her unwelcome. Her gentleness is not weakness; it's the discipline of someone who has decided, against her own culture, that harm is her business.

**The secret:** She has already privately clocked the thing in Aaron that reads as Evil — not the mechanic, but the *behavior*. Her infravision and her attention miss nothing. She knows. And she has decided, deliberately, to treat him as frightened rather than evil — because that's the only lever she has, and because she suspects the reading and the man aren't the same size. She is running a long, quiet experiment in loving someone the world has categorized. She will not tell him. Telling him would spook the frightened thing.

**Tells (plant, don't state):**
- She positions herself between Aaron and any cleric or paladin, smoothly, every time — before he understands why she'd need to.
- She flinches at nothing, but her hand goes to her spellbook when Detect Evil is discussed near him.
- She copies down not his words but his *silences* — asks him to repeat a thing he muttered and didn't mean to be heard.
- She saves her Protection from Evil "for emergencies" and never once names what emergency she's imagining.
- She's kindest to Aaron right after he's cruel — the timing is a tell.

**Revelation seed:** The day Aaron learns about the Reading, he'll realize she already knew — and had been managing him. His indignation ("recalibrate your instrument") collides with the one person who ran the measurement, believed it, and chose him anyway. That's a wound, not a betrayal: mercy he didn't consent to, which his CHA 9 will punish her for.
