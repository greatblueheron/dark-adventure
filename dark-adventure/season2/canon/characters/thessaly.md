# PUBLIC PERSONA

Thessaly is a tall elf gone lean and weathered in a way elves usually aren't — a face that's stopped bothering to be beautiful. She keeps her hair short and practical, cut with a knife by feel. She wears her armor like a second skin and rarely takes it off, even to sleep. Her hands are scarred across the knuckles. She moves quietly by default; Aaron notices she's *arrived* before he heard her cross the floor, and it bothers him every time.

Her manner is spare. She doesn't fill silence. She listens, she watches the exits, she waits. When she does speak it's short, flat, and usually correct in a blunt, unornamented way. She isn't cold like Aaron is cold — she's *economical*, and the difference reads, to the party, as calm. Under pressure she gets calmer. When violence comes she's already moving, and it's over faster than anyone expected, and she's cleaning her blade before the others have drawn.

**Voice for TTS:** Lower-mid alto, dry and unhurried. A slight rasp, like a voice that talks less than it could. Flat affect — not menacing, just level. Neutral accent, faintly archaic vowels. Pace slow, with real pauses she doesn't rush to fill. Never raises her volume; when she's dangerous, she gets *quieter.*

**Speech patterns:** Short declaratives. Rarely more than a sentence. Answers the practical question, ignores the emotional one. States odds and facts as if reading a weather report.

Sample lines:
- "Two guarding the door. One's asleep. I'll take the awake one."
- *(when Aldrin frets over a plan)* "It'll work or it won't. Standing here decides nothing."
- *(over a body)* "He's done. Check his boots."

**Stated backstory (what she claims):** Border scout from a woodland march that no longer exists — burned out in a war she names differently each time anyone presses, which nobody does twice. Says she's been selling her sword ever since. Doesn't romanticize it. Doesn't ask about anyone else's past, which the party mistakes for courtesy and is actually disinterest.

**Habits:** Cleans her weapon compulsively, even when clean. Eats first and fast. Sleeps in armor, boots on. Counts the party by touch and sound in the dark — Aaron has heard her doing it, a low tally under her breath, and finds it oddly reassuring until he thinks about *why* she'd need the count.

**How she treats Aaron:** With an ease no one else offers him. She doesn't find him cold — she finds him *readable*, and treats him like the one adult in a room of children. She'll stand near him in a fight without being asked. She hands him the practical truth others soften. To Aaron, starved for a person who doesn't flinch, this feels like being *seen.* It is the most dangerous relationship he has.

**Running bit:** "Check his boots." She loots the dead with brisk, unsentimental efficiency, and always starts with the boots — a practical tic the party has learned to expect, half-joking about it now. It stays funny right up until it doesn't.

# HIDDEN TRUTH

Thessaly is **Neutral Evil**, and she is the cleanest example of it in the party: no cruelty for its own sake, no chaos, no cause. She is *out for herself*, absolutely and without apology, and she has organized an entire competent life around that single axis. People are assets, threats, or weight. She keeps the party alive because a living party is a better shield and a better payday than a dead one — and not one hour longer than that math holds.

She is drawn to Aaron because he *reads legible* to her — she clocks the same self-interest under his analyst's dryness, the shaded testimony he hasn't confessed. She thinks she's found her one honest peer. Her "seeing" him is real, which is what makes it poison: she'll validate exactly the parts of him that redemption would require he starve.

**Tells to plant (never state):**
- She always positions herself *behind* the party in a bad fight, framed as watching their backs. Aaron eventually notices she's never once been the one bleeding.
- "Check his boots" — she reaches the dying before the healers do. Once, someone savable dies while she's busy at his boots. She doesn't hurry.
- When a plan requires a body left behind to buy time, she suggests it first, gently, reasonably, and names the *right* member to leave.
- She counts the party in the dark — Aaron realizes it's not headcount. It's *inventory.*
- She never sleeps un-armored except once: the night before she'd already decided to be gone by morning.

**Betrayal seed:** When the numbers turn — a cornered fight, a treasure that won't split, a pursuer she can outrun by leaving one slow body behind — she will cut her losses with the same calm she cuts everything. She'll offer Aaron the exit *with* her, framed as the two survivors recognizing each other. His answer is his whole arc, spoken aloud, too late.
